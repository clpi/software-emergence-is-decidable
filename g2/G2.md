# G2 — dependence scan cost: the indexed exact scan

Status: the scan cost this gap names is closed for the scan as
the rungs instantiate it, at every registered size, with exact
results throughout. What remains open is a different quantity,
named in section 7. Landed with the rung-6 head as base; the
measured pins are in section 6.

## 1. The gap as stated

Spec section 9, gap G2: the exact dependence scan of section
1.5 is one suffix replay per message; at hundreds to thousands
of actors over long horizons this may be the battery's dominant
cost; the spec's answer is the sampled label and its
consequences, which keep sampled quantities out of every
theorem and certificate; a sublinear exact scan, if one
exists, is an open problem this spec does not assume.

The rung records carried the gap forward in the form the
batteries actually met it. Rung 2 closed its determinism gap
by replacing system replays with a replay of the recorded log
(the checker fold), so no rung ever re-ran the generating
system for a counterfactual. What the batteries pay is the
fold form of the scan: one full fold per counterfactual. Rung
3 added a second remainder of the same gap: exact sensitivity
and deadness above eleven axes, where subset enumeration over
the configuration cube stops being feasible and the rung
reported restricted-family lower bounds and no deadness at
all.

This document closes both remainders for the fold scan, and
says exactly which quantity it does not touch (section 7).

## 2. The scan as the rungs instantiate it

A view is computed from a recorded delivery log. The fold
appends each record's delivered values to per-key lists, one
key per scored (destination, site) pair. A component is a
function of one key's list: a held-out estimator (median in
the field family, mode in the region family) scored against
the site's truth. Components sum over a group's keys.

Three counterfactual families are taken over the fold:

- Tick counterfactuals (the hyperedges of section 1.3, and
  the two-sided dependence confirmation of section 5.2): the
  fold with one tick's deliveries removed, one fold per tick.
- Message counterfactuals (section 1.5 read literally): the
  fold with one record removed, one fold per record.
- Subset counterfactuals (sensitivity, liveness, deadness):
  the fold with a configuration's complement removed, one
  fold per configuration subset — two to the axis count,
  capped in rung 3 at eleven axes with a registered
  subfamily above that.

The baseline cost at a registered instance is therefore
(folds) times (one full fold plus one full component
evaluation): hundreds of folds at rung 2, hundreds of
component evaluations per view at rung 3, and a fold per
record for the message family.

## 3. The observation

The fold never re-runs the system, and its appends commute:
a key's final list is the multiset of its delivered values,
and every component is a function of that multiset alone.
A counterfactual fold is the same multiset with a named part
removed — the records of one tick, the values of one record,
the values of the masked-out source groups. So every
counterfactual the scan takes is a function of one indexed
pass over the log, plus removals. No refold is ever needed,
and no quantity is sampled: every value below is the exact
value the baseline computes, in exact rational arithmetic
where the baseline compares floats for equality (field scores
are integers over a denominator of 120; region scores are
zero or one).

One structural fact shapes the message family: a record
delivers to a single destination, so at the fold a single
record's removal can move only that destination's group
component. Per-message dependence edges are therefore
singletons or empty — never engaged in the sense of section
1.3, which needs an edge of size at least two. The tick unit
is the unit that produces engaged edges. This is a fact
about the fold, stated here because the cost result depends
on it: the message family costs one leave-one-out estimator
per (key, record) pair, not one fold per record.

## 4. The indexed scan

One pass builds, per scored key: the full value multiset; the
multiset partitioned by tick; the multiset partitioned by
source group (in rung-3 level-2 views, level-1 values are
ungated background, exactly as the rung's include functions
define); and the per-record values. From the index:

- Tick edges: for each key and tick, the estimator of the
  multiset minus that tick's part, computed from the sorted
  list (field) or the count table (region) by removing the
  part. A component changes at a tick exactly when one of its
  keys' scores changes; the changes sum in exact rationals.
- Message dependence: for each key and record in it, the
  estimator of the multiset minus that record. A record is
  dependence-bearing exactly when its destination's group
  component changes.
- Subset scores: for each key, its score under every subset
  of the source groups present at that key (background fixed),
  from the partitioned multisets. A configuration's component
  is the sum of its keys' subset scores.
- Sensitivity, decided exactly at any axis count: fix an axis
  and a component. The toggle difference — the component at a
  configuration with the axis on, minus the same
  configuration with it off, as a function of the remaining
  axes — is a sum over the component's keys of functions of
  the small group subsets present at each key. A function on
  a subset cube is identically zero if and only if every
  coefficient of its multilinear expansion is zero (Moebius
  inversion over the subset lattice: the coefficients are
  the alternating sums over subcubes, and the expansion
  reconstructs the function from them). The scan sums the
  per-key coefficients monomial by monomial in exact
  integers. The axis is sensitive for the component exactly
  when some summed coefficient is nonzero, and the smallest
  nonzero monomial names a witness configuration: the
  difference at that monomial's configuration is the
  coefficient sum over its sub-monomials, and the scan
  returns the witness with the sensitivity verdict. Where
  the baseline enumerated the whole cube (at most eleven
  axes), this test is the same decision by a cheaper route;
  above the cap it decides what enumeration could not.

Liveness, the full-configuration emergence count, and the
interaction matrix follow from the edges and the exact
sensitivity exactly as the baseline defines them. Deadness —
liveness unchanged with one axis removed from the full
configuration — is decided by the same test, at any axis
count.

## 5. Exactness

Three lemmas carry the construction, all proved in the
companion Lean file (core only, no sorry, axioms within
propext and the quotient soundness axiom):

- Removal commutes with the fold: the entries of the log
  with one tick's records removed are the tick-filtered
  entries of the whole log, since every entry carries its
  record's tick. (The same statement for a single record is
  the filter partition below applied to that record's
  entries.)
- A sum splits over any predicate's filter partition, so a
  component's change under a counterfactual is the sum of
  its keys' changes; untouched keys cancel exactly.
- The coefficient criterion in its one-variable and
  two-variable cases, proved in full; the general case is
  the Moebius inversion argument of section 4.

Executed controls, all at the pins of section 6:

- The rung-2 engine reproduces the persisted small instance
  view on every field, including the full float components,
  and the from-scratch baseline port's output on every field
  at the main and probe instances.
- The sensitivity decision agrees with brute-force subset
  evaluation on every rung-2 instance (eight axes, the whole
  cube enumerated as a cross-check inside the engine run).
- The per-message family agrees with a literal brute-force
  implementation — one full fold per record, no index — on
  the small instance: 115 dependence-bearing records of
  4,096, identical edge sets.
- The rung-3 engine reproduces the persisted views' edges,
  full components, interaction matrices, liveness and
  emergence counts on all eighteen views (two batteries,
  three kinds, three formation ticks). Its exact sensitivity
  equals the recorded restricted-family sensitivity on all
  nine field views and strictly extends it on five of nine
  region views — the recorded values were lower bounds, and
  in those five views the bound was not tight; liveness and
  the emergence count are unchanged everywhere, since the
  added sensitivities are redundant at the full
  configuration. Random-mask spot checks of the indexed
  subset scores against direct entry scans: 216 checks, no
  failures.

No battery outcome is reinterpreted by any of this: every
registered verdict stands exactly as recorded. One recorded
quantity changes status, not value: deadness at the rung-3
joint views, recorded as not evaluable, is now evaluated
(section 7).

## 6. Measured cost

Instrument: Python wall clock (the interpreter's monotonic
performance counter) on this machine, with operation counters
as the stable measure; wall seconds on a shared host move
with load, the counters do not. Baseline is the rungs' own
algorithm, ported faithfully (the port reproduces the
persisted small view on every field before any timing is
taken from it). Before: repo head 8eb2906b. After: this
landing.

Rung-2 form (one view per instance):

| instance | records | baseline | indexed |
|---|---|---|---|
| small (64 actors, 32 ticks) | 4,096 | 8.6 s; 289 folds; 555,520 record applications; 289 component calls | 0.8 s, all families |
| main (512 actors, 48 ticks) | 49,152 | 83.3 s; 305 folds; 7,375,984 record applications; 305 component calls | 8.5 s, all families |
| probe (2,048 actors, 32 ticks) | 131,072 | 252.0 s; 289 folds; 17,838,080 record applications; 289 component calls | 32.3 s, all families |

The indexed column includes the per-message family and, at
the probe instance, the brute-force sensitivity cross-check.
The message family alone, taken literally at the small
instance, costs 115.7 s of brute force — more than the whole
baseline view — and is included in the indexed column's 0.8 s
at no separate line. Dependence-bearing records: 115 of
4,096 (small), 853 of 49,152 (main), 3,326 of 131,072
(probe).

Rung-3 form (nine views per battery):

| battery | axes | baseline | indexed |
|---|---|---|---|
| field joint | 19 | 79.7 s wall (24.4 s user) | 9.9 s |
| region joint | 29 | 196.5 s wall (54.1 s user) | 18.8 s |

The growth the gap feared — folds times log size, subsets
times axes — is replaced by one indexed pass plus per-key
work proportional to what each key actually holds: at the
probe instance, 151,628 indexed entries, 124,014 key-tick
pairs and 87,638 subset scores decide the whole view,
against 17.8 million record applications for the baseline's
folds alone. Exact sensitivity at 29 axes costs 100,907
coefficient terms across the battery's nine views; the
baseline's enumeration route would need over five hundred
million configurations at that axis count and was never run.

## 7. What remains, and what this does not claim

- The system-level scan. Section 1.5 defines dependence by
  re-running the generating system with a message removed.
  No registered battery uses that form — rung 2 replaced it
  with the checker fold, and every later rung kept the fold —
  so its cost is not a cost any rung pays. If a future
  registration reinstates system replays, that cost is a new
  quantity to measure then; this landing neither measures
  nor bounds it. G2's remaining content is exactly this
  sentence.
- The highest-minimal table above the enumeration cap.
  Liveness, deadness and the emergence count are exact at
  any axis count under this scan. Enumerating every minimal
  sufficient set at 19 or 29 axes is the rung-6 solver's
  registered territory (the F1 family), not the dependence
  scan, and is not claimed here.
- Deadness, evaluated. With exact sensitivity at the joint
  views' axis counts, the rung-3 level-2 dead lists are:
  field — all blocks but one at the first formation tick
  (block 10 lives), all but blocks 5 and 6 at the second,
  all but blocks 8 and 11 at the third; region — all but
  blocks 0, 2, 6, 13 at the first, all but blocks 0, 8, 13,
  16, 18, 20 at the second, all but blocks 1, 11, 21, 22, 27
  at the third. So the antecedent of the rung-3 group
  condition — every group actor emergent-dead at level 2 at
  every formation tick — evaluates, and is false in both
  families: at every formation tick some block is not dead.
  The condition itself stays a premise, for the reasons the
  premise audit recorded (its discharge route is the rung-6
  soundness theorem in general form, not a scan); what
  changes is only that its antecedent is no longer
  unevaluable at these axis counts.

## 8. Files and reproduction

In this directory: the indexed engines for the rung-2 and
rung-3 view forms, the faithful baseline port used for the
before measurements, the literal per-message brute force
used as a control, the Lean companion, and the measured
outputs. Reproduction: run the baseline port and the engine
over the same persisted log and compare the outputs field by
field; run the brute force against the engine's per-message
output on the small instance; compile the Lean file cold
with the pinned toolchain and print the axioms of its
theorems.
